   SERVICE. Preserve the pullback_lab folder. Do not delete existing files/data.
2. Change the service start command to: python pullback_app.py
3. Keep OANDA_TOKEN secret and OANDA_ENV=live.
   Set PULLBACK_OUTPUT_DIR=/data/p32_pullback_pass1
   Set PULLBACK_LEGACY_CACHE_DIR=/data/p32_signal_admission
   Keep the persistent volume mounted at /data.
4. Redeploy, then open /health. Confirm:
   service=P32_PULLBACK_PASS1_V1_2026_10_20
   persistence.separate_mount_detected=true
   orders_supported=false
5. Open /status to start; refresh for progress.
6. Once COMPLETE, download /results.zip and send it back.

The study is resumable. Historical downloads can take a long time and require
several GB of persistent disk. It runs the baseline/discovery stage only.
